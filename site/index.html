Easier than ever, from open data

Modelling a city's streets, buildings and traffic by hand used to keep a team of 3D artists busy for months. Today most of it is open data that anyone may download: OpenStreetMap's streets, lanes, signs and trees, mapped by volunteers; building footprints and heights from national registers and Overture Maps; terrain from lidar and global elevation models; the weather and the sun of a real day; bus timetables; open traffic counts. Free, open simulators, CARLA for the 3D world and SUMO for the traffic, turn it into a world you can drive.

Coupled CARLA puts these together, and the map maker reduces it to typing a place and pressing a button. It is meant for people who do not program: researchers in psychology and human factors, designers, students, city planners.

What it does

Make a map of any place

Type a town, a square, an address or coordinates, in any language. Coupled CARLA downloads the streets from OpenStreetMap, prunes the road graph so a car can always drive on, and writes an OpenDRIVE map for CARLA with a matching SUMO network. It guesses the driving side from the country and the traffic from the size of the town, and fills the map with buildings at their real footprints and heights, street trees, lamps, benches, signs, parked cars, real terrain and the bus lines with their timetables. A medium map takes 3 to 5 minutes, most of it waiting for the open data services.

Make a map · step-by-step guide

Coupled experiments

Several people share one world, as in coupled-sim: a driver (keyboard or a Logitech G25 with force feedback), a pedestrian, an AV passenger who sets the car's eHMI, a drone pilot, each at a station on any machine of the lab network. Scripted AI cars follow routes with coupled-sim-style triggers and show eHMIs on the front, rear, roof, road or as eyes that follow a pedestrian. SUMO traffic yields to pedestrians. Visual sync, round trips and frame latency are logged; analysis measures gaps, time to collision and PET for every encounter.

Digital twins on CARLA 1.0

Central Eindhoven baked into a CARLA 1.0 (Unreal Engine 5.8) level from Dutch open data: AHN terrain, the 8 cm PDOK aerial photo on the ground and roofs, 3DBAG buildings with real roof shapes and facade styles by construction year and use, lane markings, kerbs and sidewalks. It runs in real time with SUMO traffic and stations. The same pipeline makes a twin of any place from the best open data of its country (Salamanca, Shibuya and Leuven tested). The Eindhoven twin is a downloadable content pack.

The Eindhoven digital twin · a twin of any place · download

Drones

A participant can fly a quadrotor with a gamepad, keyboard or RC transmitter, and AI drones fly deliveries, swarms, patrols and hovers, all with coupled-sim's flight model ported to numpy: rotor thrust, a PX4-style controller at 500 Hz, turbulence from the real wind and a battery model. Delivery routes keep their band above the roofs and go round tall buildings at SORA clearances.

Drones

Sound, in-car displays and eHMIs

Each station hears the world in calibrated dB SPL from published acoustic models: CNOSSOS-EU sound power per vehicle, synthesised engines, AVAS on electric cars, reflections off the real facades, ISO 9613 propagation, binaural output for headphones, and an ambience that follows the real weather and season. Drivers and AV passengers get an instrument cluster, a HUD and a centre screen on the car's own panels; eHMIs and haptic cues come from coupled-sim's design files.

Sound, displays and eHMIs

Session dashboard

Run a whole session from a browser on the host or a tablet: choose the experiment or make a map of a new place, give each station in the lobby its role, start trials on every station at once, and watch a live map over an open aerial photo with every participant, AV, SUMO vehicle and traffic light. Set eHMIs, weather, time of day and take-over requests live; open finished trials in the viewer or download them.

Session dashboard

CARLA's towns

One line, map: {carla_town: Town10HD_Opt}, drives CARLA's own towns with every feature: SUMO traffic that runs the towns' own traffic lights, parked cars, a crowd and CARLA's AI walkers, stations, eHMIs, drones, sound and night lights. Town01 to Town05 and Town10HD passed a smoke test with no collision.

Standard CARLA maps

Photorealistic tiles (optional)

On a baked twin, map.photoreal: google streams Google's Photorealistic 3D Tiles over the level with Cesium for Unreal, while cars keep driving on CARLA's roads and walkers on its sidewalks. It needs your own Google Maps Platform key with billing; the server caps the requests per day and month. Without a key the trial runs on the normal twin.

Real traffic

SUMO drives everything that is not a participant: EIDM car following, cyclists on the real cycle network, car fleets from open vehicle registers, demand fitted to open traffic counts, and the real bus lines at their GTFS frequencies, live where agencies publish positions.

Traffic · public transport

19 cities

Eindhoven plus three cities per inhabited continent, from Tokyo and Cairo to Luang Prabang and Ushuaia, left- and right-hand traffic, each with its scenery, terrain and bus lines; all 19 pass a smoke test.

Logging and analysis

coupled-sim-style CSV at 50 Hz, events, live JSON over UDP, SUMO's trajectories of the whole map, CARLA's recorder, and a browser trial viewer (three.js) that replays any trial in 3D.

Quick start

Without programming

  1. Download Coupled CARLA (ZIP) and unpack it.
  2. Install uv, then, in the coupled-carla folder: 
    uv venv --python 3.12 .venv
uv pip install -e ".[carla,maps]"
  3. To drive: unpack CARLA 0.9.16 as a folder CARLA_0.9.16 next to coupled-carla.
  4. Double-click make_map.bat (Windows), Make a map.command (macOS) or make_map.sh (Linux), type a place, press Build this map, then Drive it.

The map maker, step by step

From a terminal

git clone https://github.com/bazilinskyy/coupled-carla.git
cd coupled-carla
uv venv --python 3.12 .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
uv pip install -e ".[carla,maps]"
export CARLA_ROOT=~/CARLA_0.9.16

coupled-carla-map "Markt, Eindhoven"     # find, build, summarise
coupled-carla-run experiments/places/<name>.yaml   # the name it printed
coupled-carla-run experiments/eindhoven_centre.yaml \
    --input autopilot --headless --duration 60
coupled-carla-session --experiment experiments/eindhoven_session.yaml

The python -m coupledcarla.<module> commands of the README work as well. Driving needs a Windows or Linux PC with a gaming graphics card; maps can be made on a Mac too. A Docker image for the map maker and the SUMO tools: docs/docker.md.
